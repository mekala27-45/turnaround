# Weather attribution

Hourly weather at the FAA Core 30 airports from the Open-Meteo historical weather API,
https://open-meteo.com/, pulled once per airport and year in UTC.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The values are ERA5 based reanalysis at the
airport's coordinates, not the airport's own observations.
